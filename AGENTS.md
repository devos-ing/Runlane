# Development scope

- Before planning or implementing a feature, refactor, or dependency change, read [Development scope](content/development-scope.md). Reuse the work item's scope record and completion evidence.
- Use [CONTEXT.md](CONTEXT.md) for terminology and [Decisions and delivery](content/decisions.md) to distinguish agreed direction, recommendations, and planned work. Planned capabilities do not expand the current task's authorization.
- For verification, follow the [verification policy](content/decisions.md#verification-policy). AI agents must not write, run, or delegate unit or end-to-end tests.
